# Stage 3 (the visitor assistant): questions for Isaac

Answer by voice, by letter, or "default" (the first choice). A build session asks these with multiple-choice buttons
and records the answers in DECISIONS.md. Nothing here is decided until Isaac answers.

## A. What visitors really ask (the test set, 20+ needed)
1. Say, in your own words, the questions people ask you most: at shows, by text, by email, on Instagram. Aim for 20; more is better. (Voice is perfect.)
2. Which of these come up often? (pick all) a) price b) size or will it fit my wall c) colour to match a room d) shipping e) commission timeline f) materials / outdoor use g) care and cleaning h) installation / hanging i) payment plans j) trade / designer pricing k) seeing it in person.
3. The hardest or oddest question a buyer ever asked you?

## B. How it should sound
4. Voice: a) warm and brief b) gallery-formal c) like you talking d) plain and factual.
5. Answer length on a phone: a) 1–2 sentences then pieces b) a short paragraph c) longer when asked.
6. Call you: a) "Isaac" b) "the artist" c) "the studio" d) "Isaac Anderson".
7. Emoji or exclamation marks: a) never b) rarely.
8. Words you never want used (e.g. "decor", "affordable", "stunning")?

## C. What it may say
9. Prices: a) only listed prices, otherwise "price on request" b) also a range for commissions (you give it) c) never prices, always you.
10. Commission price range to quote, if any (e.g. "most commissions $3,000–$15,000")?
11. Commission timeline to quote: a) 8–12 weeks (old site) b) another number c) never, always you.
12. Deposit for commissions: a) 50% (platform default) b) another % c) don't mention yet.
13. Shipping answer until checkout exists: a) "flat rate for small works, a quote within two working days for large" (D-102) b) always "ask Isaac".
14. Returns: a) seven days on available works, none on commissions (D-102) b) don't mention.
15. Outdoor use, weight, hanging hardware: a) only what a piece's record says b) general facts you give it now (tell them).
16. Discounts or holds: a) never offered; "ask Isaac" b) it may offer a 48-hour hold request (you approve).
17. Trade / designer pricing: a) "yes, ask Isaac for trade terms" b) a stated trade discount (%) c) don't mention.
18. Studio visits: a) by appointment, ask Isaac b) not offered.
19. Where your work can be seen in person (shows, galleries, cities)?

## D. Handing off to you
20. When it can't answer, it offers: a) text / email / call (all three) b) email only c) text first.
21. Collect the visitor's name and contact before handing off: a) only if they offer b) always ask c) never.
22. Tell you about every conversation, or only ones with a lead? a) only leads b) a daily summary c) every one.
23. How you want leads: a) text and email b) email only c) text only.
24. Hours it should say you reply in: a) "within a day" b) "within two working days" (old site) c) other.

## E. Limits and safety
25. Off-topic questions (weather, other artists, homework): a) politely back to the art b) short answer then back.
26. Questions about other artists or galleries: a) never comment b) neutral facts only.
27. Visitors per day before it slows them down: a) default (20 messages each) b) stricter c) looser.
28. When the $10 monthly budget runs out: a) plain search plus "ask Isaac" and you're told (plan) b) stop the chat entirely.
29. Model: a) Haiku 5.5, cheapest (default, D-102) b) Sonnet 5.5, better and about 3x the cost.
30. Language: a) English only b) answer in the visitor's language.

## F. The extras
31. "Open these as a page" (a saved page of pieces it suggested): a) yes in stage 3 b) later.
32. Save chats across devices with an email: a) yes b) later.
33. Show a piece "on my wall" from chat: later (stage 5); OK? a) yes b) want it sooner.
34. First thing the assistant says (greeting)? Default: "I'm the studio's assistant, an AI. Ask about any piece, size, colour or commission."
35. Anything it must always mention (e.g. "every piece is one of a kind")?
